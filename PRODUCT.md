# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who dictate into other applications, record meetings, and review their saved conversations.
They need to return to their words without understanding recognizers, speaker labelling, or file formats.

## Product Purpose

Voice is Local provides on-device dictation, meeting transcription and review, and reading aloud.
Speech and meeting processing stay on the Mac. The interface makes recording, opening a meeting,
correcting its words, and exporting a result clear.

## Brand Personality

Local, practical, clear. Use plain labels and familiar native macOS controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid exposing internal processing stages as competing navigation choices. Avoid several buttons
that open different representations of the same meeting without explaining the difference.

## Design Principles

- Give a task one clear entry point and keep its navigation predictable.
- Show processing state without making the person choose a processing-specific view.
- Keep frequent actions visible and place advanced maintenance actions in a secondary menu.
- Preserve local processing, explicit destructive-action confirmations, and user edits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard navigation, accessible control labels, semantic system colors, and readable
system fonts. Meeting opening works through a button, Return, double-click, and a context menu.

The current feature and interaction contracts are in docs/design.md and docs/meeting/.
